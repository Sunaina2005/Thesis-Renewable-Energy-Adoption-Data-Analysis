Key Finding
Interpretation of GDP–RE relationship
Strong Correlation
The GDP–RE relationship shows a strong positive correlation (r = 0.71), but with important nuances about scale vs. per-capita performance.
Policy Effect
Policy variables (FIT, RPS) add significant explanatory power beyond GDP alone — ΔR² = +0.18.
Super-Proportional Scaling
GDP elasticity of 1.24 (p < 0.001) suggests RE investment scales super-proportionally with economic growth.
